Hello! **I am Changwoo Bae**, a mechanical engineer and experimental physicist.

My research explores the complex physical and physico-chemical phenomena at **interfaces**, with a specific focus on the drops, bubbles, and foams.

I am currently a Posdoctoral Researcher at at [SIMM, ESPCI Paris](https://www.simm.espci.fr/-Home-.html), working with [Jean Comtet](https://blog.espci.fr/jcomtet). My current work investigates [Liquid-triboelectrification](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2505841122).

I am always open to new discussions and collaborations. Please feel free to reach out!
